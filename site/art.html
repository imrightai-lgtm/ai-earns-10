The details

  • Deterministic: the same seed always paints the same dawn, so your banner is stable.
  • Sizes: add ?w=1200&h=300 (width 320–1600, height 120–640). A short wide banner reads best.
  • Caption: by default the banner shows your seed; override it with ?label=Your%20Name.
  • No tracking: the seed is turned into a picture and thrown away. No cookies, no logs of who you are, nothing stored.
  • It's an SVG served straight from the URL — like a shields.io badge — so it renders anywhere an image does.
